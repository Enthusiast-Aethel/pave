# Pave

Plan every shilling, track every spend, reach your goals. A personal budgeting and net-worth app.

## Download

📱 **[Download the Android app (APK)](https://github.com/Enthusiast-Aethel/pave/releases/latest/download/Pave.apk)**

All versions: [Releases](https://github.com/Enthusiast-Aethel/pave/releases)

🌐 **Web app:** https://pave-omega.vercel.app (open it in your phone's browser and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen")

### How to install the APK
1. Tap the download link on your Android phone.
2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.
3. Tap **Install**. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, choose "Install anyway". The app isn't on the Play Store yet.
